Milan Župunski

Plant biologist working on cell signaling, stress physiology, and quantitative approaches to complex biological systems.

Over fifteen years of international research experience across Germany, Austria, and Serbia, spanning plant cell signaling, stress physiology, phytoremediation, and nanoparticle biostimulants. I also co-founded an agri-biotech venture and co-invented a national patent for seed-priming technology.
